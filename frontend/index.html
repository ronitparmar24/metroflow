Everything you need to know about MetroFlow

After booking a ticket through your dashboard, a unique QR code is generated instantly. Simply scan this QR code at the metro station entry gates — no paper tickets needed. The QR code is valid for the date and route you selected.

Navigate to the Wallet section in your dashboard and click "Recharge". You can add any amount between ₹50 and ₹5,000. The balance reflects instantly and can be used for booking tickets, purchasing monthly passes, or topping up your Metro Card.

Tickets can be cancelled up to 30 minutes before the scheduled departure time. A full refund is credited back to your MetroFlow wallet within seconds. Expired or used tickets are not eligible for refund.

The MetroFlow Card is a virtual smart card linked to your account. It stores your balance, monthly pass, and travel history. You can use it for quick tap-and-go entry at stations without needing to book individual tickets each time.

Yes. All passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 hashing, and sessions are managed securely with server-side cookies. We never store plain-text passwords, and all API communications are protected. Your transaction data is stored in an encrypted MySQL database.

Monthly passes give you unlimited travel between any two stations for 30 days. They're significantly cheaper than buying daily tickets. You can purchase one from your dashboard and it activates immediately. Your pass status is always visible in the Metro Card section.
